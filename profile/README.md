# Sequence

**Climbing training software. Know exactly what to train. Know it's working.**

Sequence helps you plan, log, and review your climbing and training in one place.

Build structured sessions, track performance over time, and watch the progress show up on the wall.

Train on your own, from self-guided plans, or directly with a coach.

## What we build

- **Web app**: plan and schedule training, set and track goals, build sessions with exercises, log routes and boulders (outdoor and indoor) in your logbook
- **Mobile app**: log sessions on the go, inbuilt timer, synced with the web app
- **Coaching tools**: program and manage athletes' schedules, share templates, follow progress
- **Plans and workout packs**: get expert training plans and workout packs from coaches like Climb Strong, then add them to your schedule or library and make them your own

Sequence is _designed for climbing_. Workouts, goals, projects, hangboard timers, exercises (sets/reps/weight/duration), logbooks, metrics, and rest days are built in. A flexible widget system covers everything else, so a session can track whatever you want. Third-party integrations (e.g. Strava, theCrag) and our MCP connector let you take it to yet another level.
